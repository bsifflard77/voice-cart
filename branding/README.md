## 🖼️ Logo Variations

### Icon Only (Square)
- **Use:** App icon, favicon, social media profile
- **Sizes:** 1024x1024, 512x512, 192x192, 144x144, 96x96, 72x72
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background

### Horizontal Logo (Icon + Text)
- **Use:** Website header, email signatures
- **Format:** SVG, PNG
- **Variations:** Light background, dark background

### Vertical Logo (Icon over Text)
- **Use:** App splash screen, business cards
- **Format:** SVG, PNG

## 📐 Logo Design Elements

### Shopping Cart
- Simplified shopping cart silhouette
- Two wheels at bottom
- Open basket design
- Blue color (#0EA5E9)

### Sound Waves
- 7 vertical bars representing audio waveform
- Positioned above/within cart
- Varying heights for dynamic feel
- Represents voice activation

### Typography
- **Font:** Inter or similar sans-serif
- **Weight:** Bold for "VoiceCartz"
- **Style:** Modern, clean, friendly

---

## 📱 Icon Specifications

### PWA/App Icon Requirements
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Color:** Blue circle (#0EA5E9) with white icon
- **Safe Area:** Keep important elements within center 80%
- **Sizes Required:**
  - 1024x1024 (App Store)
  - 512x512 (Android)
  - 192x192 (PWA)
  - 144x144 (Windows)
  - 96x96 (Standard)
  - 72x72 (Small devices)

## 🎯 Usage Guidelines

### DO:
✅ Use the official logo files provided
✅ Maintain minimum clear space around logo
✅ Use approved color palette
✅ Scale proportionally
✅ Use on contrasting backgrounds

### DON'T:
❌ Alter logo colors
❌ Distort or stretch logo
❌ Add effects (shadows, gradients, etc.)
❌ Use old VoiceCart branding
❌ Crowd the logo with other elements
