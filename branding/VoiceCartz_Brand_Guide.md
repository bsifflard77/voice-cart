
# VoiceCartz Brand Guide

## Overview

**Brand Name:** VoiceCartz\
**Domain:** VoiceCartz.com\
**Tagline:** Talk. Shop. Done.

VoiceCartz is an AI-powered shopping companion that allows users to
build and manage shopping lists entirely through voice. Designed for
speed, simplicity, and hands-free convenience, the brand embodies
innovation, clarity, and trust.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Brand Essence

**Core Idea:** Turn your voice into your cart.\
**Mission:** To simplify shopping through intuitive voice interaction
and smart AI organization.\
**Tone:** Helpful, confident, approachable, and modern.\
**Personality Traits:** - Smart and intuitive\
- Friendly and conversational\
- Efficient and reliable\
- Fresh and modern

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Logo System

### Primary Logo

-   **Composition:** Shopping cart with vertical sound-wave bars forming
    the basket.\
-   **Typography:** Sans-serif, geometric typeface (Inter or Poppins).\
-   **Color:** Deep blue gradient (#0066FF → #3B82F6).\
-   **Usage:** Default logo for digital, print, and presentation
    materials.

### Secondary Logos

1.  **Horizontal Version:** Icon to the left, text on the right. Ideal
    for website headers.\
2.  **Vertical Version:** Icon above the text. Great for mobile splash
    screens or app launches.\
3.  **Icon-Only Version:** Simplified circular symbol with the soundwave
    cart---used for app icons, favicons, and social avatars.

### Clear Space & Sizing

Maintain a clear area around the logo equal to the height of the "V" in
VoiceCartz. Minimum size for digital use is **72x72px**.

## Typography

**Primary Font:** Inter\
Modern, legible, open-source, excellent for web and mobile use.

**Alternate Font:** Poppins\
Used for headings or marketing materials where a softer tone is
preferred.

  Usage       Font             Weight
  ----------- ---------------- --------
  Headlines   Poppins Bold     700
  Subheads    Inter SemiBold   600
  Body Text   Inter Regular    400
  Captions    Inter Medium     500

## Visual Elements

### Iconography

-   Use rounded, minimalist icons.\
-   Prefer solid or outline styles consistent with Material Design.\
-   Maintain color consistency with primary blues and neutrals.

### Emojis & Visual Tone

Use emojis sparingly in social posts to communicate friendliness and
ease: 🛒🎙️💬✨\
Examples: - "Add to your 🛒 with your 🎙️ --- it's that easy!" -
"VoiceCartz makes shopping ✨simple✨ again."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Brand Applications

### App Icon

-   Circular background in **Rich Blue (#0066FF)**.
-   Centered white cart-soundwave symbol.
-   Designed for clarity at all scales (from 1024x1024px down to
    72x72px).

### Website & Digital

-   **Header:** Horizontal logo on white background.\
-   **Footer:** Icon-only version on dark blue background (#1F2937).\
-   **Buttons & CTAs:** Gradient blue with white text.

### Social Media

-   Use the icon-only logo for profile images.\
-   Cover photos should feature the tagline and subtle soundwave
    graphics.

### Stationery & Collateral

-   **Business Cards:** Blue logo on white front; reversed white logo on
    blue back.\
-   **Email Signature:** Include logo icon, name, title, and website.\
-   **Slide Templates:** Gradient blue header bar with logo top-left.

## Final Notes

VoiceCartz branding is designed to be simple, voice-forward, and
scalable. Whether seen as an app icon, a social profile, or a startup
pitch deck logo, it should always communicate ease, intelligence, and
approachability.

**Consistency Tip:** Always use the same logo proportions, blue hues,
and type hierarchy to reinforce brand trust and recognition.
